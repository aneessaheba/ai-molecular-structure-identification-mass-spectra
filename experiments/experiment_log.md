## Failed / Ineffective Approaches
_For each: what was tried, what happened, why we think it failed, and what we learned._

### Cyril: E101/E102 standalone fingerprint prediction

Both final MLPs underperformed the measured same-context library baseline (0.418836 and 0.471017 vs 0.891360 MRR@25). E102's richer features improved MRR by 0.052181 over E101, but did not replace direct spectral matching. Validation compounds occur in the reference library through other acquisitions, favoring library search; fingerprint prediction may also discard information useful for distinguishing candidates. These explanations have not been isolated experimentally. E103 showed complementary signal: alpha 0.2 improved validation MRR to 0.895501, whereas alpha 0.5 reduced it to 0.888956. The weight was selected on the same 250 validation molecules, so the gain needs independent/Kaggle confirmation. Kaggle did not confirm it: on the public leaderboard E103 scored 0.137 against the baseline's 0.135, inside the leaderboard noise, and the two choose the same top-1 for all 400 test molecules. E101 (0.089) and E102 (0.098) kept their local order and stayed well below the baseline. See [final results and figures](../members/mrcyrilgoud/README.md#results-and-hand-off) and [Kaggle results](../members/mrcyrilgoud/README.md#kaggle-results).
