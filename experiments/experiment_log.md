## Failed / Ineffective Approaches
_For each: what was tried, what happened, why we think it failed, and what we learned._

### E511: same-adduct bonus on entropy similarity (Mrnidhi)
- **What was tried:** raise a reference spectrum's entropy similarity by 10% or 30% when it has the same adduct as the query spectrum.
- **What happened:** MRR@25 fell from 0.9294 to 0.9248 (10%) and 0.9276 (30%).
- **Why we think it failed:** the best match is already taken over all same-mode reference spectra, so a same-adduct spectrum wins on its own merit when it really is the closest. The bonus mostly lifts weaker matches of wrong candidates that happen to have the same adduct. The differences are also small enough to be noise on 250 molecules.
- **What we learned:** adduct information is better given to the LightGBM ranker (E502) as a separate feature than forced into the similarity score by hand.

### E502: LightGBM ranker scored below the entropy scorer it uses as a feature (Mrnidhi)
- **What was tried:** a LightGBM LambdaRank model on 15 features per candidate (the E501 entropy score, the E002 cosine score and its rank, matched peaks, library and formula features), trained on 7,134 molecules from three other libraries with their own library hidden from the reference.
- **What happened:** MRR@25 = 0.9107 against 0.9294 for E501 alone. The right answer lost first place on 12 validation molecules (10 fell to 2nd, two to 3rd) and gained it on 3. Top-10 went up from 98.4% to 99.6%. On the training libraries the ranker was also behind the entropy score alone (0.9468 against 0.9546).
- **Why we think it failed:** a tree model scores each candidate from its own row, so a feature that says where the candidate stands in its list is worth more to it than an absolute score. The only such feature was the rank by cosine, and it took 85% of the gain (`members/Mrnidhi/figure_importance.png`). That cosine is the E002 one, which looks at a candidate's first 50 spectra only, so the ranker mostly followed the weaker ranking. Without the rank feature the ranker was lower still on the training libraries (0.9445). The formula features are the same for isomers, which are 91% of the baseline's misses, and took under 3% of the gain.
- **What we learned:** the ranker needs the rank and the gap to the best candidate for the entropy score too (planned as E503). Feature choices are judged on the leave-one-library-out check first, so the validation set is not used for tuning.
