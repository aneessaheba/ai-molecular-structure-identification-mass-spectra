## Failed / Ineffective Approaches
_For each: what was tried, what happened, why we think it failed, and what we learned._

**E702: Model 2, spectrum to SELFIES transformer (hide_true MRR 0.034)**
- **Tried:** a transformer that encodes the peaks and decodes SELFIES tokens, trained 15 epochs on the Kaggle T4 GPU (best validation loss 0.453, validation token accuracy 0.836). At test time, beam search (beam 25) produced about 109 candidates per molecule.
- **What happened:** every beam was a valid molecule, but only 6.9% of valid beams fell within 15 ppm of the true mass. 57.6% of molecules had at least one in-mass beam, 55.2% had the right molecular formula, and the true structure was recovered for 5.6% of molecules. The mean best Tanimoto similarity to the true structure was 0.38.
- **Why we think it failed:** (1) The decoder does not condition on the precursor mass, so the mass filter removes about 93% of beams. (2) The model learns general chemistry but rarely reproduces the exact structure. A Tanimoto of 0.38 means close, not exact. (3) The model is under-trained: validation loss was still falling at epoch 15. (4) We trained on at most 2 spectra per structure to fit the time limit, which limits how much each molecule is seen.
- **What we learned:** producing valid SMILES is not the bottleneck. Mass control and exact structure recovery are. Mass-conditioned decoding and longer training are the obvious next steps, but we did not do them because they are outside the course time.

**E701 (Model 1, plain analog enumeration): normal-mode cost.** Hidden-true MRR is 0.238, but normal MRR drops from 0.877 to 0.851. The generated products push some correct library hits down. This is a real trade-off, not a free gain.

**E710: flat down-weighting of products (gen_weight 0.5 and 0.3).** This protects normal molecules (0.870 and 0.874) but loses about half of the hidden-true gain (0.125 and 0.090 vs 0.238). It is a plain trade-off, not a fix.

**E711: confidence gate at τ = 0.7.** This is a better trade-off than E710 (hidden-true 0.207, normal 0.861). It is still a small loss on normal molecules, and τ was tuned on the same 250 validation molecules, so the gain is optimistic.
